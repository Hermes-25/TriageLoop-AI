# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TriageLoop.ai is a research and learning project. We expect respectful, evidence-oriented collaboration. Participants must communicate constructively, welcome correction, protect privacy and distinguish prototype evidence from clinical claims.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, reproducible feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and limitations;
- respecting different backgrounds and levels of technical experience;
- protecting personal, patient and security-sensitive information; and
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, publication of private information or knowingly misleading clinical claims are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related communication. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the competition platform or another private contact published by the project team. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled proportionately, with confidentiality where practical.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.
