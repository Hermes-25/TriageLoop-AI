# Security Policy

TriageLoop is a synthetic research prototype and must not receive real patient data.

## Supported scope

- Use only the included synthetic fixtures or similarly deidentified test data.
- Do not expose the local prototype to the public internet without authentication, authorization, TLS, secrets management, network controls and deployment-specific review.
- The role label is a workflow demonstration, not production access control.
- The SHA-256 chain is tamper-evident prototype linkage, not immutable storage.

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue containing credentials, personal data or vulnerability details. Contact the repository owner privately through the competition platform/team contact listed in the public repository.

## Deployment gate

Any use beyond a local synthetic demonstration requires clinical-safety review, threat modeling, privacy/legal review, validated identity and role controls, encryption and key management, retention policy, monitored audit storage, incident response and prospective local clinical validation.
