# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Emergency-department triage nurses are the primary users. They work under interruption, time pressure, variable lighting and fluctuating capacity while maintaining awareness of many waiting patients at once. Charge nurses need the same operational truth at queue and resource level. Competition evaluators need a separate evidence view that makes assumptions, limitations and measured simulation results inspectable.

## Product Purpose

TriageLoop is a nurse-led, deadline-aware triage operating system for emergency-department waiting rooms. It turns each patient's changing condition into a conservative Dynamic Action Window, compares that clinical requirement with the queue's predicted time to action, and exposes the resulting Clinical Slack. It asks for the most decision-useful next observation when uncertainty matters, preserves clinician control, and records every material transition.

Success means a nurse can answer, within seconds: who needs attention next, by when, why the recommendation changed, whether the department can meet it, what to measure next, and what will be recorded if the recommendation is accepted, modified or overridden.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, humane. The product should feel like a well-maintained clinical instrument: serious without being intimidating, dense without feeling cluttered, and confident without pretending certainty.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI dashboards built from interchangeable metric cards, gradients, oversized risk scores and decorative charts.
- Consumer wellness aesthetics, glassmorphism, glowing neon or “AI magic” motifs.
- Conventional hospital software that is visually noisy, spreadsheet-like without hierarchy, or dependent on memorized color codes.
- Autonomous decision language that hides uncertainty, capacity failure or clinician responsibility.
- Alert-heavy workflows that interrupt for every model output and train users to ignore the system.

## Design Principles

1. **Deadline before score.** Lead with the required action, latest recommended time and Clinical Slack; probability remains supporting evidence.
2. **One patient, one consolidated signal.** Combine rules, trajectory, uncertainty and capacity into a single actionable row instead of multiplying alerts.
3. **Capacity truth stays visible.** Never let ranking imply that an infeasible queue has become safe.
4. **Explain in the workflow.** Show what changed, why it matters, reliability and Next Best Observation beside the decision—not behind a generic “explain AI” button.
5. **Human authority is a first-class state.** Accept, modify and override are explicit, legible and auditable; no autonomous downgrade is possible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the web prototype. Meaning is never carried by color alone; urgency uses label, icon/shape and ordering. All primary workflows support keyboard operation, visible focus, readable zoom/reflow, reduced motion, descriptive labels and non-visual summaries for charts. Dense clinical tables may scroll horizontally where necessary but preserve row identity and a usable narrow-screen patient view.
